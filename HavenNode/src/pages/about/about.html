About HavenNode

A community-centered hazard mapping and navigation guide for the municipalities and barangays of Benguet Province.

⚠

Hazard Mapping

Identifies and visualizes hazardous zones across Benguet — including landslide-prone slopes, flood corridors, and earthquake fault lines — using updated geospatial data.

🗺

Map Guide

Serves as an interactive map guide to help residents, travelers, and local officials navigate roads, evacuation routes, and key landmarks across the province.

🛡

Community Safety

Built with and for Benguet communities — empowering barangay leaders, DRRM officers, and everyday citizens with real-time awareness of risk areas.

Our Mission

HavenNode was created to address the unique geohazard challenges of Benguet's mountainous terrain. The Cordillera highlands face frequent risks from typhoon-induced landslides, flash floods along river valleys, and seismic activity — yet many communities lack accessible, up-to-date hazard information.

We bridge that gap by combining official PHIVOLCS, MGB, and PAGASA data with local knowledge, making it available through an intuitive map interface anyone can use — online or offline.

Built for Benguet

HavenNode is an open initiative developed to support disaster risk reduction and management (DRRM) efforts in the Cordillera Administrative Region. We work alongside local government units, CDRRMO offices, and civil society to keep data accurate and communities informed.
